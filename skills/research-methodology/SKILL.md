# Research Methodology — Decision-Relevant Uncertainty

**Enter before substantive research reasoning or external discovery**, not merely before writing an experiment. Preserve the current research owner's design goal, state and authority. This is a research **decision/workflow**, not a literature/evaluator/statistics/operator implementation skill. Do not preload adjacent design, coding, execution, evaluation or closure skills because they may become relevant later.

**Research law:** aim for the smallest defensible decision, one named active uncertainty at a time. Each substantive action identifies (1) design goal, (2) current unresolved question, (3) smallest discriminating evidence action and (4) how each possible outcome narrows the frontier or yields **STOP**. If the action cannot alter a named uncertainty, do not run it.

## Research routing — load only the current state

- **Current research / literature comparison:** Start with the active owner/roadmap and directly relevant existing evidence. Classify prior results `EXACT_REUSE | PARTIAL_REUSE | STALE`; preserve closed and falsified branches. External papers and candidates must answer a named residual gap with a non-duplication delta and expected decision-relevant information gain; novelty or topic relevance is insufficient. Read [discovery frontier](references/discovery-frontier-workflow.md) **only** if maintaining or changing uncertainty state, nominating an experiment/candidate, or resolving an ambiguous owner/frontier. For a single literature source, conditionally use `references/literature-qualification.md`; do not restart the research program.
- **New or materially changed experiment plan — mandatory authoring gate:** Before drafting, creating, promoting or authorizing a new experiment, **reload the current owning roadmap/spec, latest directly relevant memo/guide, directly relevant terminal/sealed evidence and tested-candidate census, and active uncertainty node**, even when read earlier in this thread. Bind scope/version/production-context, ruled-out hypotheses, `EXACT_REUSE | PARTIAL_REUSE | STALE`, one discriminating action and outcomes in the existing research owner. `PRE_EXPERIMENT_CONTEXT_GREEN` only when these agree; otherwise `PRE_EXPERIMENT_CONTEXT_NOT_READY`: repair the owner evidence, leave the plan in backlog, and do not activate it. See [exact authoring gate and uncertainty-node fields](references/discovery-frontier-workflow.md#mandatory-pre-experiment-context-gate) **only at authoring**, not when executing a frozen active plan.
- **Running a qualified experiment or interpreting its results:** Require three explicit parts: **claim/benchmark evidence coverage** (population, positive/negative/boundary and protected vs development cases), **auditable comparison** (independent-enough reference and evaluator, frozen denominators/controls/metrics, unknowns and invalid runs), and **outcome-to-decision loop** (possible retain/falsify/inconclusive/STOP states, attribution, bounded re-evaluation). Freeze protected evidence rules **before** reading results. Choose finite budget and the smallest discriminating experiment. Load [experiment qualification](references/experiment-qualification-workflow.md) only for protocol/evaluator/training/interpretation detail; specific research services and training references remain conditional.
- **Terminal interpretation / closeout:** Keep falsified, negative and inconclusive findings with exact scope and evidence; do not silently reopen them. Attribute a result among data/reference, evaluator, implementation, representation, model, routing/workflow and policy before recommending adaptation. Update only the existing roadmap/memo/discovery state with qualified evidence, eliminated/retained hypotheses, claim limits, comparable re-evaluation status, and **zero or one** proposed next question. Read [closeout and attribution](references/closeout-and-attribution-workflow.md) only at that state or for a disputed causal claim. A successor may be nominated but is **not automatically authorized**.

## Scientific and authority invariants

- **Evidence is bounded and temporal:** preserve actual source, version, input/population, model/evaluator, contract, code/config, result/reduction and time identities *as required by the claim*. Reuse existing receipts and Git/test evidence; do not create a second ledger or one artifact per PASS. Evidence reported today is not evidence produced under today's contract. `UNKNOWN`, disputed references, invalid apparatus, actor failure and abstention are distinct; never silently convert them into zero, omit denominators or infer real independence from simulations/replays.
- **Measurement integrity:** an aggregate score or reproducible procedure alone does not establish validity or attribution. Do not tune repeatedly on protected evidence. Changes to labels, benchmark, scorer, ontology or acceptance basis require a versioned comparable basis or an explicit non-comparability disposition; improvement requires direct comparable evidence, not a narrative, code change or simulated success.
- **No authority promotion:** research conclusions, discovery projections, real-user corrections and candidate improvements are **not** automatically executable rules, production admission/activation, normative authority, deployment or permission for another experiment. Scientific `UNKNOWN` is not a governance block unless an actual protected boundary requires it.
- **Specialists on demand:** select literature review, evaluator qualification, statistics, bounded training, research services or engineering design **only** when that task/state is entered. The current research skill owns the next research decision; the specialist owns its operation. Do not load all of them together.
- **Stop condition:** when the decision has sufficient evidence or no next experiment has positive decision value, **STOP** and record the bounded disposition. No open-ended candidate sweep, automatic fan-out, research restart per conversation or unapproved model spend. Negative results may close the exact research scope without delivering the outer product goal.

The smallest useful record belongs in the **existing** owner (not a new file): `design goal → active uncertainty → reused/ruled-out evidence → discriminating action/decision → result/provenance/limits → zero-or-one next question or STOP`. For an already active governed plan, consume its frozen refs and revalidate only changed dependencies; **never re-run the pre-experiment authoring census merely because execution, verification, or a new conversation began**.
