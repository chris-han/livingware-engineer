# Intervention Placement and Research Search

Use this reference when agent improvement could target evidence/context, execution scaffolding, model behavior, or research-search diversity. Reuse the owning plan and evaluation contract; do not create another ledger or an autonomous optimization loop.

## Diagnose before choosing the lever

Apply the existing model-training qualification before nominating parameter learning. For harness or search changes, inspect the same material alternatives proportionately:

- **Evidence and representation:** determine whether the required facts, dependencies, scope, polarity, and relationships actually reach the actor.
- **Execution and harness:** distinguish blocked valid actions, parsing/interface defects, repeated actions, premature termination, and exhausted budgets.
- **Content and discrimination:** inspect quality on comparable valid completed cases with sufficient evidence, rather than attributing every low score to the model.
- **Search diversity:** investigate whether comparable independent trajectories find materially different solution families while one lineage repeatedly refines the same approach.

Treat the first observed failure signal as a diagnostic entry point, not an identified root cause. Preserve overlapping signals and unresolved attribution. A rising share of content failures may reflect repaired process failures exposing previously hidden cases; compare counts, denominators, and common completed cases before claiming content regression.

Separate changes to **what the actor sees** from changes to **what the actor does or writes**. Record the intervention surface and the evidence needed to attribute its effect. A plateau, aggregate gap, or unsuccessful non-parametric recipe does not establish a universal harness ceiling or a requirement to train.

For tasks whose declared scoring gives valid non-delivery zero, report delivery rate and quality among delivered results separately; their product is the aggregate score. Do not apply this decomposition to invalid apparatus, disputed references, or semantic UNKNOWN by silently scoring them zero.

## Cross harness and weights when transfer is the question

When claiming that training internalizes a harness benefit, compare the four relevant cells on one frozen evaluation basis:

| | Base weights | Candidate weights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original harness | Baseline | Weight transfer |
| Candidate harness | Runtime contribution | Combined effect |

Estimate weight transfer, runtime contribution, and interaction using comparable tasks, operational budgets, and uncertainty reporting. Reuse valid cells when their identities remain comparable; do not require four expensive runs for unrelated research. Development evidence selects treatments; protected evidence confirms the bounded claim.

Keep information-bearing context mechanisms unless direct evidence shows they are dispensable. Training does not establish that unavailable task-specific evidence can be removed. Distillation of behavior also does not authorize removal of governance, deterministic verification, or permission boundaries.

Calibrate measurement noise for the actual task and environment. Do not copy a paper's numeric improvement threshold, rollout count, or LoRA recipe as a universal default. Search-winner scores need independent remeasurement or a declared selection-aware procedure, and broader improvement claims need protected confirmation.

## Optional fresh-context branch exploration

Treat Fork-and-Flush as an **opt-in research-search candidate**, not a default workflow, recovery ritual, or source of extra compute authority. Use it only within an authorized finite experiment whose named uncertainty concerns exploration diversity. Updating this reference does not authorize a behavioral-learning run.

At a declared checkpoint:

1. Preserve an immutable workspace basis, verified artifacts, provenance, negative results, evaluator/split identities, remaining budget, and authority constraints.
2. Start isolated continuations with fresh conversational contexts. Retain the task and binding facts/constraints; allow hypotheses and solution explanations to be formed again. Inspect retained notes for assumptions that could preserve the same anchoring.
3. Give each continuation the declared exploration allowance. Account for total model tokens/cost, evaluator calls, tool/runtime and relevant training compute across all branches; lower wall time is not lower total cost.
4. Select under the frozen development protocol, independently recheck the winner as required by that protocol, and continue only within the existing authorization. Preserve losing evidence when it prevents repeat work.
5. Emit a bounded candidate or STOP. Branch selection is not implementation approval, model admission, activation, or authorization of a successor experiment.

Parallel alternatives for one uncertainty are treatment replicates inside one owned experiment, not several speculative successor questions. Preserve the root skill's one-next-question-or-stop rule and existing parallelism permissions.

Before promoting this strategy to a default, run the smallest decision-relevant matched-budget comparison of continuous research, context reset only, branching only, and branching plus reset when those cells are needed to identify the mechanism. Freeze schedules and selection rules in advance. Include selection cost and unchanged correctness/retention obligations; report inconclusive outcomes without automatic further search. Do not copy fixed fan-out counts or cadence from external experiments without local justification.

## External evidence and adoption limits

- Yuan Tian, Bing Hu, Hao Wang, Binghang Lu, and Fang Wu (2026), *Harness Evolution Hits a Ceiling: When Weight Training Should Begin*, arXiv:2610.11655v1, https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.11655. Supports using failure composition and a harness-by-weights comparison to investigate intervention placement. DeepPlanning results show transfer of some behavioral gains; WebArena-Lite provides a counterexample where added page visibility was not reproduced by training. The reported ceiling depends partly on the diagnostics exposed to the proposer; first-signal labels do not establish causal ownership. These benchmark results do not validate this workflow or a project's training need.
- Ziyang Cai et al. (2026), *Fork-and-Flush: Escaping Idea Basins in Autoresearch Agents*, arXiv:2610.07447v1, https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.07447. Supports testing workspace-preserving fresh-context branching as a search-diversity intervention. Matched-budget experiments and fork-only/flush-only ablations support the candidate on the studied tasks. Aggregate gains use min-max normalization; functional-similarity maps are supporting evidence, not proof of a causal landscape. Results on strong coding agents do not establish benefit for small local models, legal decisions, or Livingware's default workflow.

Adopt the diagnostic and evaluation guidance as methodology. Keep universal ceiling claims, automatic training switches, and default Fork-and-Flush effectiveness as unvalidated hypotheses. Preserve external evidence, project synthesis, measured local benefit, and authority transitions as separate claims.
